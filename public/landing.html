Most sign language resources leave you checking your own homework.

They're scattered across textbooks and video clips, and there are only so many fluent teachers around to tell you if a gesture actually lands. SignWise watches your hands while you practice.

How it works

Four steps, every lesson.

01

Turn on your camera

SignWise runs in your browser and uses your webcam. Nothing to download or install.

02

Watch a sign

See each gesture demonstrated clearly before you're asked to try it yourself.

03

Sign it back

Mimic the gesture in front of your camera, at your own pace.

04

Get instant feedback

Know right away whether it matched, and what to adjust if it didn't.

What's inside

Built around practicing with your hands, not a mouse.

Camera-based hand recognition

Your webcam detects and tracks your hand shape in real time, the same way it tracks a face for a video filter.

Real-time feedback

Every attempt is scored the moment you make it, with a clear note on what to fix if it's not quite right.

Interactive learning mode

Watch the correct gesture demonstrated, then practice imitating it right alongside the reference.

Quiz & practice mode

Answer quizzes by performing the sign itself.

Progress tracking

See which lessons you've finished and how your quiz scores are improving over time.

Every sign is progress.
